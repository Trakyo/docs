Trakyo can only credit what it can see. These are the habits that keep what it sees close
to what really happened, in rough order of how much difference they make.

## Send every visitor through a tracked link

Credit starts at the click. Someone who hears your offer and types your domain into their
browser arrives untracked, and whatever they buy later lands under **Other**. Use a
tracked link in every description, every email, every ad, every pinned comment.

## One link per placement

A link shared between two videos, or two ads, can tell you that the pair worked. It can
never tell you which one. Make a new link for each placement and name it after that
placement.

## Keep the link intact

Tracked links carry the visitor's identity in the address. Anything that rewrites the
address can throw it away: another shortener in front of a Trakyo link, a redirect that
drops everything after the `?`, a page that "tidies up" its own address. If a placement
sends real traffic but never records clicks, this is usually why.

## Record payments taken off-platform

Bank transfers, cash and invoices paid by hand do not reach Trakyo on their own. Decide
who records them and when — most teams do it weekly. Revenue nobody records is revenue
your leaderboard credits to nothing.

## Watch the Other bucket, not just the leaderboard

**Other** is your error bar. A small amount is normal. If it grows, something in the
chain between the click and the payment is dropping the visitor's identity. Check it
weekly, before you make decisions on the leaderboard.

## Agree on one attribution mode for decisions

First touch and last touch answer different questions, and both are correct. Trouble
starts when two people compare numbers taken in different modes. Pick one as your default
for reporting, and treat switching as a deliberate act of analysis.

## Give it time before you judge a number

Dashboard figures trail reality by a few minutes, and journeys play out over weeks. A
video that looks dead after two days may be the one filling your funnel next month.
