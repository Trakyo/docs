Anything you can put a link in can be tracked: a paid ad, an email, a newsletter, a
podcast description, a QR code on a flyer. Each one becomes its own row on your content
leaderboard, next to your videos.

:::note[Before you start]
- The Trakyo script must be installed on the pages the link points at. See
  [Install the tracking tag](/docs/install).
:::

## Step 1: Create the link

1. In Trakyo, open your links.
2. Create a new link to the page you want people to land on.
3. Give it a name you will still recognise in three months — the placement, not just the
   channel. "Newsletter #42 footer" beats "newsletter".

## Step 2: Use it wherever that placement appears

Paste the Trakyo link in place of your normal URL.

:::tip
One link per placement, not per channel. Two ads sharing a link look like one row, and
you lose the only thing you wanted to know: which of them worked.
:::

## Step 3: Check it worked

1. Click the link yourself.
2. Wait a few minutes, then open the content leaderboard on your dashboard.
3. The new item should be listed with at least one click.

## Keeping the link intact

Tracked links carry information in their address. If something strips it, attribution is
lost even though the visitor arrived:

- Don't shorten a Trakyo link with another shortener.
- Don't let a redirect drop the part of the address after the `?`.
- Where you can only use a plain link, send people to a page that does carry the Trakyo
  script, rather than editing the link by hand.

If you are not sure whether a placement keeps the link intact, ask your developer to
check it against [Install the tracking tag](/docs/install).
