Your dashboard answers one question: which content is bringing you money. This article
explains each part of it, so the numbers mean the same thing to everyone on your team.

:::note[Before you start]
- The tracking script needs to be installed on your site, and your payment tool connected.
  Until both are done, the dashboard will look emptier than your real business.
:::

## The funnel

The funnel counts people at four stages, in order:

1. **Clicks** — someone arrived through one of your tracked links.
2. **Opt-ins** — they gave you their details in a form.
3. **Bookings** — they booked a call.
4. **Closes** — they paid.

Each stage counts people, not actions, so one person who books twice is still one booking
in the funnel.

## Revenue and attribution

Revenue is always credited to a piece of content, and you choose how:

- **First touch** credits whatever first brought the person in. It shows you what fills
  the top of your funnel.
- **Last touch** credits whatever they saw just before they bought. It shows you what
  closes.
- **Multi-touch** splits the credit equally between everything in their journey.

The total revenue is the same whichever you pick. Only the split between pieces of
content changes. For the longer explanation, see
[First vs last touch](/docs/attribution).

:::tip
A video with high first-touch revenue and low last-touch revenue is not underperforming:
it is a discovery asset. Cutting it would starve everything downstream.
:::

## The content leaderboard

One row per piece of content — a YouTube video, an ad, a custom item — with its clicks,
opt-ins, bookings and revenue. Sort it by revenue to see what is actually paying for
itself.

## The "Other" bucket

**Other** is revenue Trakyo could not trace back to any content. A little is normal.
A lot usually means something in your setup is dropping the visitor's details before the
sale is recorded. [Get attribution you can trust](/docs/help/best-practices/attribution-best-practices)
covers the usual causes.

## A note on timing

Dashboard numbers can trail reality by a few minutes. If a sale from two minutes ago is
missing, wait and refresh before treating it as a problem.

## Check it worked

Pick yesterday as the date range. If you had clicks, opt-ins or sales yesterday, every
stage of the funnel should show a number and the leaderboard should list at least one
piece of content.
