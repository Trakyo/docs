Trakyo shows which piece of content made you money: every click, form, booked call and
payment is traced back to the video, post or ad that brought the person in.

Your numbers are only as good as your setup. Before you read the dashboard, do these three
things, in this order:

1. **Install the tracking tag** on every page of your site.
2. **Connect your forms and calendar** so Trakyo sees each new lead.
3. **Connect your payment processor** so revenue lands on the right content.

:::warning
If a step is missing, revenue still arrives but can't be traced, and shows under **Other**
on the dashboard. That looks like Trakyo isn't working when it's really a setup gap.
:::
