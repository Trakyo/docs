Every person who gives you their details becomes a lead. Their journey is the list of
everything Trakyo saw them do, in order, so you can answer "where did this customer
actually come from?" for one person rather than in aggregate.

## Step 1: Find the person

1. In Trakyo, open **Leads**.
2. Search for their email address or name.
3. Open their record.

## Step 2: Read the journey

The journey is a timeline, oldest first. A typical one reads:

1. Arrived from a tracked link (the video, ad or email that brought them in).
2. Came back later, perhaps more than once.
3. Filled in a form.
4. Booked a call.
5. Paid.

The first entry is what first-touch revenue credits. The last entry before the payment is
what last-touch revenue credits.

:::note
A lead can exist with no clicks before their opt-in. That means they reached you without
passing through a tracked link, so there is nothing to credit and their revenue shows
under **Other**.
:::

## Notes

Use notes to record anything your team should know about the person — what they asked for
on the call, why they bought. Notes are for your team only; they never change the numbers.

:::danger
Never paste anything into Trakyo that you would not want a colleague to read. Notes are
visible to everyone with access to your account.
:::

## Check it worked

Open a lead you know personally — someone who bought after seeing a specific video. The
first entry in their journey should be that video.
