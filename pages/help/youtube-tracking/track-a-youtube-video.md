A YouTube video only earns credit in Trakyo if viewers reach your site through a link
Trakyo can recognise. That link is what ties a sale weeks later back to the video that
started it.

:::note[Before you start]
- The Trakyo script must be installed on your site. See [Install the tracking tag](/docs/install).
- You need to be able to edit the video's description, or the link in your pinned comment.
:::

## Step 1: Create a link for the video

1. In Trakyo, open your links.
2. Create a new link pointing at the page you send viewers to.
3. Name it after the video, so you recognise it on the leaderboard later.

Use **one link per video**. A link shared between two videos cannot tell you which of
them earned the sale.

## Step 2: Put the link under the video

1. Open the video in YouTube Studio.
2. Paste the Trakyo link into the description, and into your pinned comment if you use one.
3. Save.

:::warning
Use the Trakyo link everywhere you mention that offer for this video. A viewer who
reaches your site by typing your domain in arrives untracked, and their revenue ends up
under **Other**.
:::

## Step 3: Check it worked

1. Open the link yourself from the video description.
2. Wait a few minutes, then look at the content leaderboard on your dashboard.
3. The video should now appear with at least one click.

From then on, every opt-in, booking and payment from people who arrived that way is
credited to the video automatically.
