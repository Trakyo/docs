## Step 1: Open Integrations

1. In Trakyo, click **Integrations** in the left sidebar.
2. Find **Stripe** and click **Connect**.

![The Integrations page with the Stripe Connect button](./connect-stripe-1.png)

## Step 2: Approve the connection

1. Sign in to Stripe with an admin login.
2. Click **Allow**.

A pro tip that saves the reader time.
